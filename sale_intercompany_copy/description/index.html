Sale Order — Copy to Another Company

Stop re-entering quotations manually across your Odoo companies. Copy any sale order to another company in one click — with full traceability.

🏢 Multi-company ⚡ One click copy 🔗 Full traceability 📋 Bulk copy

Key Features

Everything you need to manage quotations across your Odoo companies

⚡

One Click Copy

Select any sale order and copy it to another company in one click. All lines, prices, discounts, sections and notes are transferred automatically.

🔗

Full Traceability

Every copy is logged in the chatter with a direct link to the copied order. The source order also shows a counter of all its inter-company copies.

📋

Bulk Copy

Copy multiple sale orders at once from the list view. Select all the orders you need, choose the target company and copy them in one action.

🏢

Access Control

Only companies the user has access to appear as targets. No risk of copying to a company you shouldn't access — fully secured by Odoo's native access rights.

📑

Sections & Notes

Sections and notes are copied with the correct sequence — the target order looks exactly like the source, including all structural elements.

💱

Tax Auto-Assignment

Taxes are automatically reassigned based on the target company's configuration — no manual tax adjustment needed after copying.

Simple Copy Wizard

Select the target company, choose whether to copy notes, and click Copy Order. That's all it takes to duplicate a quotation across companies.

Full Traceability in the Chatter

Every copy is logged automatically in the source order's chatter — with the target company name, date, user, and a direct link to the copied order.

How It Works

Three steps, immediate result

1

Open the action menu

From the sale order form or list view, click Action → Copy to Another Company.

2

Select the target company

Choose which company should receive the copy. Only companies you have access to are shown.

3

Click Copy Order

The order is duplicated instantly with all lines, taxes, and traceability. A confirmation message shows the new order reference.
