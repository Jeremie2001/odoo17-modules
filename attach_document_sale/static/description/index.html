Auto-Attach Product Documents to Sales Emails

Never forget to send product files again!

This module automatically attaches all product-related documents (catalogs, specifications, images, PDFs, etc.) when you send a quotation or sales order by email. Simple, fast, and error-free.

Before vs After

❌ Before (without module)

The email composer only includes the quotation PDF. Product files are missing.

✅ After (with module)

The email composer automatically includes all product-related documents.

How It Works

1. Attach documents to your products

Each product can have technical sheets, catalogs, or images stored as attachments.

2. Create a quotation with these products

When you send the quotation by email, the module automatically retrieves all product documents and adds them to the email.

Benefits

  • Automatic product document attachment
  • No more forgotten files
  • Save time for your sales team
  • Professional and complete emails for customers
