📎 Auto-Attach Product Documents to Sales Emails

Never forget to send product files again!

The challenges you face:

  • Manually attaching product documents to every sales email?
  • Forgetting important product catalogs or specifications?
  • Time-consuming search for the right product files?
  • Inconsistent documentation sent to customers?

✅ The solution:

This module automatically attaches all product-related documents (catalogs, specifications, images, PDFs, etc.) when you send a quotation or sales order by email. Simple, fast, and error-free.

Key Features

  • 🤖 Fully automated - No manual intervention required
  • ⚡ Time saver - Eliminates manual file attachment process
  • 📎 Smart detection - Finds all product attachments from quotation lines
  • 🎯 Perfect customer experience - Complete documentation every time
  • 🔧 Zero configuration - Works with existing product attachments
  • 📧 Seamless integration - Works with standard Odoo mail composer

📋 How It Works - 3 Simple Steps

1

Attach documents to your products

Add catalogs, specifications, images to your product templates. You probably already have these files!

2

Create quotations normally

Create your sales quotations with products that have attached documents. No changes to your workflow!

3

Send by email - Magic happens!

Click "Send by Email" and all product documents are automatically attached. Your customer receives everything!

💡 Real-World Example

Scenario: You're selling industrial pumps. Each pump has:

  • Technical datasheet (PDF)
  • Installation guide (PDF)
  • Parts catalog (PDF)
  • Product images (PNG/JPG)

Result: When you send a quotation for 3 different pumps, all 12 documents (4 docs × 3 pumps) are automatically attached. Your customer receives everything they need!

Installation & Setup

  1. Install the module from Odoo App Store
  2. That's it! No configuration needed
  3. The module works immediately with your existing product attachments

💡 Pro Tip: If you don't have product attachments yet, just go to your products and add catalogs, specs, or images in the attachments section. The module will find them automatically!
