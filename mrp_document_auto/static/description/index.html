Advanced Sales Margin Management

Control your profit margins with automatic calculations and a powerful analytics dashboard.

📊 Analytical dashboard ⚡ Real-Time Calculations 🎯 Project tracking 🌍 Multilingual

Key Features

Everything you need to optimize your sales margins

💰

Automatic Calculations

Margin in amount and percentage calculated automatically on each order line. Updated in real time when changes are made.

🌐

Overall Margin

Manage margins at the order level with automatic redistribution across lines. Unique and innovative functionality.

📊

Analytics Dashboard

View your KPIs: total margin, revenue, average percentage, number of orders and projects. Modern and interactive interface.

📈

Interactive Charts

Margins over time, performance by sales rep, top projects. Powered by Chart.js for a smooth experience.

🎯

Default Margin

Automatic application of a 35% margin on new products. Customizable according to your business needs.

⚠️

Smart Validations

Automatic alerts for invalid margins, missing costs, or inconsistent percentages. Avoid costly mistakes.

💵

Discount Management

Calculation of reduced margins taking into account discounts and promotions. Clear overview of the impact of discounts.

👥

Sales Performance

Analyze the performance of each sales representative: margin generated, number of orders, conversion rate.

🔍

Top Projects

Identify your most profitable projects with automatic ranking by margin, revenue, and volume.
